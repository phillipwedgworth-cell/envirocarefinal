Common Questions

Termite Protection FAQ.

How much does Sentricon® cost?

Pricing varies based on your home's size and perimeter. Our annual protection starts at $360/year for typical Alabama homes. Initial installation is included. We provide an exact quote after the free inspection — no surprises.

What if I already have termites?

Sentricon® works for both prevention and active infestations. The bait eliminates existing colonies, then ongoing protection prevents new ones. We document the elimination so you have proof for insurance, real estate, or peace of mind.

How long does the system last?

As long as you maintain annual service, your protection is continuous and your $1M repair coverage stays active. Many of our customers have been protected for 20+ years on the same Sentricon® system.

Will Sentricon® work in clay soil or under concrete slabs?

Yes. Stations are installed in the soil around your home's perimeter, not under the slab. Clay, sand, and rocky soils are no problem. We've installed thousands of systems across Alabama's varied terrain.

What's a WDO letter and do I need one?

A Wood Destroying Organism letter is a formal inspection document required by many lenders before closing on a home purchase. As a Sentricon® Certified Specialist, we provide WDO letters for real estate transactions throughout Alabama.

Why not use liquid treatment instead?

Liquid termiticides require trenching and drilling, inject chemicals into your soil, and only create a barrier — they don't eliminate the colony.

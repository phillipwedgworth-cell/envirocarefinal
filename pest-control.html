Common Questions

Pest Control FAQ.

How much does pest control cost?

$70 every 2 months for typical Alabama homes. That's $420/year for 6 treatments. Larger homes (4,000+ sq ft) or properties with extensive outbuildings may run higher — we'll quote you exactly before any work starts.

Do I need to be home for service?

No. We treat the exterior perimeter, so you don't need to be there. If you want interior treatment that visit, just let us know in advance. We send reminders before each scheduled service.

What if I see bugs after a treatment?

Call us. We come back free. Unlimited re-service is included in your plan — there's no deductible, no service fee, no minimum interval. If you see pests, we treat. That's the deal.

Is it safe for my kids and pets?

Yes, once the application dries (about 30 minutes). The products we use are EPA-registered and applied at concentrations far lower than what's used for one-time fumigations. After it dries, the yard and treated surfaces are safe for normal use.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There's no annual contract. We earn your business every two months. Most of our customers have been with us for 5-15+ years because the service works — but you're never locked in.

Why not monthly service?

Monthly is unnecessary for prevention. The products we apply maintain effectiveness for 60+ days on protected surfaces. Monthly service costs nearly twice as much for marginal additional benefit. Some companies push monthly because it doubles their revenue — not because you need it.

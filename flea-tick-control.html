Common Questions

Flea & Tick FAQ.

How much does the Elite Combo cost?

Pricing starts at $60/month for typical residential yards. Larger properties or those with extensive wooded areas may run higher. Free quotes — we'll measure and price exactly before any work happens.

What ticks do you treat?

All Alabama tick species: Lone Star, American Dog, Brown Dog, Deer (Black-Legged), and Gulf Coast ticks. We focus on the harborage zones where they wait for hosts — not random yard spraying.

Can I use this if my pet has fleas already?

Yes — yard treatment is half of the equation. The other half is treating your pet directly (vet products like Frontline, NexGard, etc.). Without yard treatment, fleas keep re-infesting from outside no matter what topical you use on the dog.

How long until I see results?

Adults are knocked down within 24-48 hours. Lifecycle interruption (preventing the next generation) takes 2-4 weeks to show full effect. Most customers report dramatically fewer pets bringing ticks/fleas inside within the first month.

What about Lyme disease and other tick-borne illness?

Alabama has confirmed cases of Lyme, Rocky Mountain Spotted Fever, and Alpha-Gal Syndrome (the red-meat allergy). Yard treatment dramatically reduces tick exposure on your property — but always do a tick check after time outdoors, especially for kids.

Is this safe near a lake or stream?

Yes, with adjusted protocols. State regulations restrict application within 25 feet of waterways, but we treat the rest of the property using lake-safe practices. Many of our most committed customers are Lake Martin homes.

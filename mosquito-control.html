Common Questions

Mosquito Control FAQ.

How much does mosquito treatment cost?

Pricing starts at $45/month and varies based on yard size, vegetation density, and proximity to water. New customers get 50% off the first application. Most Alabama yards run $45-$85/month for the 7-month season.

When should mosquito treatments start?

April. Mosquitoes start breeding when overnight temps stay above 50°F consistently — usually mid-to-late March in Alabama. Starting your barrier program in April gets ahead of the population before it explodes in May and June.

Do I need to be home during the treatment?

No. We treat the exterior only. Just make sure pets are inside and any patio furniture or play equipment you don't want sprayed is moved or covered. We send a heads-up before each visit.

How long until the yard is safe for kids and pets?

Once the product dries — usually about 30 minutes after application. After that, normal yard activity is fine. The treatment binds to leaves and surfaces; it doesn't keep migrating.

Will mosquito treatment kill bees and butterflies?

We avoid spraying flowering plants and time treatments to minimize impact on pollinators. The application targets resting/harborage areas — not blooms or open lawns where pollinators forage.

What about Lake Martin homes — does it work near water?

Yes, with adjusted protocols. We don't apply within 25 feet of the shoreline (state regulation), but we treat the rest of the property — porches, dock approaches, wooded areas, and shrubs. Many lake homes are our most committed customers because the alternative is unbearable.
